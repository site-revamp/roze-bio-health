Our philosophy

A holistic approach to health.

Founded in 2013, ROZE focuses on sustainable, preventive and protective medicine. Our approach brings healthcare and dentistry together, recognising the relationship between oral health and overall well-being.

We value thoughtful treatment planning, biocompatible care and a multidisciplinary approach that considers each patient's individual needs.

Care with accessibility in mind

Introducing Faircare®.

ROZE's Faircare initiative aims to provide access to dental care for qualifying individuals. The programme covers 90% of medical expenses for eligible UAE residents who work in the UAE and earn less than AED 3,500 per month.
