# ROZE BioHealth and BioDental Clinics – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

ROZE BioHealth and BioDental Clinics is a multidisciplinary healthcare provider in Dubai, founded in 2013 by Dr. David Roze. The company offers biological dentistry, integrative medicine, wellness and aesthetic treatments across three clinic locations: Jumeirah, DIFC and The Greens.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting ROZE's integrated healthcare approach through a refined, premium clinical experience. The design communicates its BioDental, BioHealth and BioAesthetics services while making treatment discovery and clinic enquiries easier.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Service Discovery and Navigation
- Established three distinct service pathways: BioDental, BioHealth and BioAesthetics.
- Organised individual treatments into clearer categories.
- Improved navigation links to help visitors find relevant services.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website did not clearly establish the relationship between its healthcare categories and individual treatments, making service discovery more difficult.

### 2. Improved Patient Journey and Appointment Conversion
- Strengthened the visibility of appointment and contact calls to action.
- Improved connections between treatment information and relevant clinic locations.
- Created clearer pathways from service exploration to patient enquiries.

**Problem addressed:** Contact and booking information was distributed across different sections, making it harder for visitors to identify their next step.

### 3. Stronger Visual Hierarchy and Content Presentation
- Improved the presentation of ROZE's holistic healthcare philosophy.
- Created clearer sections for clinical expertise, treatments, professional teams and clinic locations.
- Introduced more consistent imagery, refined editorial layouts and structured content blocks.

**Problem addressed:** The previous content presentation lacked sufficient visual hierarchy, making the clinic's expertise and key differentiators harder to understand.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Identity:** Introduced a sophisticated black-and-white supporting palette while retaining the established brand direction.
- **Typography:** Used refined editorial typography to establish clearer content hierarchy.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation and separation of the clinic's main healthcare categories.
- **User Experience:** Made treatment navigation, clinic discovery and appointment actions easier to access.
- **Consistency:** Established a more cohesive presentation across service sections, clinical information and location details.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a cleaner, more premium healthcare experience aligned with ROZE's biological and integrative medicine positioning.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage aims to communicate ROZE's multidisciplinary healthcare approach more effectively, simplify service discovery and guide prospective patients towards the appropriate clinic and appointment enquiry pathway.
